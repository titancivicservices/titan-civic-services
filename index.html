Our commitment beyond the contract

Equipping the youth is the next step forward.

Titan Civic Services is committed to directing 3% of our profits toward reducing opportunity gaps between young people in foster care and those growing up with consistent family support.

We believe every young person deserves access to the relationships and guidance that families and personal networks often provide. Our commitment focuses on creating meaningful connections, a sense of belonging, mentorship, and opportunities to build networks that open doors.
